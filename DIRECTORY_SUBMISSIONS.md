Use this file as the source of truth for free or low-cost directory submissions. The goal is steady, legitimate exposure and referral data, not paid listing spend or spammy backlink chasing.

## Tracking Rules

- Prefer free listings first.
- Avoid paid placements unless there is clear revenue or signup evidence.
- Use the canonical website URL: `https://www.rolearc.xyz`
- When a directory allows editable links, use UTM tracking:
  - Product Hunt: `https://www.rolearc.xyz/?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=launch`
  - SaaSHub: `https://www.rolearc.xyz/?utm_source=saashub&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=launch`
  - Futurepedia: `https://www.rolearc.xyz/?utm_source=futurepedia&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=launch`
  - AlternativeTo: `https://www.rolearc.xyz/?utm_source=alternativeto&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=launch`
- Check results weekly in Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics before adding more directories.
- Do not resubmit repeatedly after a rejection. If a site rejects RoleArc, wait until the product positioning or public proof materially improves.

## Standard Short Description

RoleArc is an AI resume builder that helps job seekers turn rough resume notes into clearer, ATS-friendly resumes with AI rewrite, clean templates, and polished PDF export.

## Standard Long Description

RoleArc helps students, entry-level candidates, career changers, and job seekers create stronger resumes without starting from a blank page. Users can choose a clean resume template, organize common resume sections, rewrite weak summaries and bullet points with AI, save drafts, and export a polished PDF when ready.

RoleArc focuses on practical resume writing and honest improvement. It does not promise interviews or hiring outcomes; it helps users present their real experience more clearly for job applications.
